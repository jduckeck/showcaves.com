Description

Grotte de Danne (Danne Cave) is actually a rock formation named Rocher Albert in honor of the president of the local Vosges Club from 1935 to 1964. This rock formation of reddish sandstone is quite spectacular and has numerous overhanging parts forming shelters and small caves. The cave is quite spectacular, obviously the weathering followoed a crack in the rock which was widened, at some places to a decents isze.

A place of refuge and remembrance, the rock that shelters the is Near Phalsbourg, the cave is located not far from the Finstingerkopf forest lodge, formerly owned by the lords of Fénétrange (13th and 14th centuries). To get there: head towards Bois de Chêne Haut, following the signs for Saverne from the center of Phalsbourg, until you reach the roundabout at Collège Saint-Antoine, then turn right onto the Bois de Chêne Haut road. Continue on Rue du Pilsfels to the end, then park at the edge of the forest and follow the signs for Grotte de Danne, passing the Finstingerkopf forest lodge. Also worth exploring is the Washhouse Trail (8.6 km – marked with a blue ring). See the "Hiking in the Phalsbourg Region" map, available free of charge at tourist information offices.
