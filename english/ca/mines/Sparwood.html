49.730993, -114.885436 The city Sparwood has numerous mine related sites which are well worth a visit. Generally we list underground tours, or at least open cast tours, but it seems those tours, which were offered every year during July and August, have been discontinued. We recieved an email that mine tours running from the Sparwood Chamber & Visitor Centre have been cancelled indefinitely due to operational changes. It seems this has not yet been published on related websites. On the other side, nearby Elkford Visitor Center offers tours of Greenhills open pit mine in July and Fording River Operations in August. The tours take place on Sundays and reservation by phone or email is mandatory. Tours are free of charge.

At Sparwood, right in the middle of the village stands a truck in a small park, which is known as Titan Truck or The Titan. It’s not a common truck, it’s the Terex Titan, the world’s largest dump truck which weighs 260 t. It is 6.90 m high, 20 m long, and 7.6 m wide, which makes it actually unsuitable for normal roads. It is able to transport 350 t of coal or rock. This truck was used in the nearby Sparwood open pit coal mine, which is operated by the Elkview Coal Corporation. It was built by General Motors of Canada in 1974.

The Sparwood Museum is nearby and is dedicated to the history of the town, its surroundings, and the mining. Pictures and artifacts are spanning over 115 years of local history. This includes both underground and open cast mining.
