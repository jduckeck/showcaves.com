Longest Caves of China


Statements about a cave being the longest whatever are quite common in show caves. Such superlatives are sometimes correct, sometimes invented, and sometimes outdated. But China has, in our opinion, a sort of weird superlative in superlative, it is the countrywith the most superlatives at all. Even with a lot of goodwill, they are mostly fake, not just outdated. But there is actually the third-longest cave of the World in China, and all in all there are several really huge cave systems. The number would be even bigger if there was more speleological exploration. Just to give a little background info, here are the longest caves of China from the official list in 2026.
