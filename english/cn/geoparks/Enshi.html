Enshi Grand Canyon-Tenglongdong Cave UNESCO Global Geopark


The name is quite long, actually typical for the baroque Chinese language. And while it covers the tower karst along Enshi River, the name also tells that it is based around the famous tourist destination Tenglong Dong. The geopark had 79 geosites in 2017, the last published list we found. Unfortunately it was uncommented, and even worse, a picture. The names were mostly not searchable, mapping tools could not finde them, a result of different transliteration and information hiding by the Chinese government. Most of the caves listed as geosites are not touristic on a national or international level, they are "only" regional. This results in the fact that they are not publicly advertised on websites. In other words, there is no possibility to find any information on location, accessibility, or geologic background.
