History

1940 begin of mining.
2000 mining company creates a theme park about the mining history of the region and the role of scheelite in the local economy.

Geology

The area has South America’s largest scheelite mineralizations, an important tungsten ore. This is a skarn-type deposit in Neoproterozoic marble, gneiss and pegmatite. Exploited were the contents of Molybdenum and Tungsten.

Description

Mina Brejuí (Brejui Mine) was for decades the largest tungsten producer in Brazil. Since 1940 it is the main site of scheelite exploitation in South America. It is also the economic backbone of the area. Currais Novos, a town of 45,000 inhabitants, was the first to have a cable TV operator in the North–Northeast of Brazil. The show mine is actually a sort of theme park presenting the mining history of the region and the role of scheelite in the local economy. It has a well-curated museum, a memorial of the founder of the mine, and offers tours into disused underground galleries.
