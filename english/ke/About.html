Kenya - About the Country


The Republic of Kenya is a country in East Africa. From the coast st the Indian Ocean low plains slowly rise to central highlands. The highlands are bisected by the Great Rift Valley. The climate is tropical along the coast, temperate inland, and arid in the north and northeast. There are four seasons, a rainy season between March and June, another between October and Noveber, and two dry seasons the rest of the year. The rainy seasons are not exactly wet, not similar to Indian monsoon, actually it is mostly moderate between the afternoons and evenings. There maybe some heavy rains too.

In a way Kenya is one of the most accessible African countries, it is open to foreign tourists, has hotels, restaurants and infrastructure for Safaris. Tourism contributed around 6% to Kenya’s economy in 2024. The reason why tourists visit the country are the landscape, spectacular volcanoes and the wildlife. There is a rather low inland tourism though. The infrastructure concerning roads is rather poor. Individual tourism is not really a good idea. All this results in the fact that there are actually very few caves open to the public. Most are actually part of a safari or guided day trip offered by a park.

Kenia has numerous game reserves, nature parks for protecting the rare animals like elephant and rhinoceros. On the other hand it has a significant amount of poaching, and the resulting security issues. Tourist groups are often accompanied by security.

And there is another problem, we were not able to find a way to define useful regions. Normally we would use the highest level governmental structure, typically "states", "regions" or "provinces". Kenya is divided into 47 semi-autonomous counties which are headed by governors, and unfortunately they are rather small and do not follow any geological boundaries at all. In other words they are not helpful for our goal. As the number of sights is actually very low, we decided to omit this kind of structuring. In other words, there are no regions on showcaves.com for this country.
