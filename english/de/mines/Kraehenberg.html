Description

The Krähenbergstollen (Krähenberg adit) forms part of a significant Sauerland silver, lead and iron ore field around the village of Bönkhausen. The best-known adits are the Churfürst-Ernst-Stollen which is the deepest adit in the “Edler Stein” mining field. In addition, there were the ‘Ottilie’, ‘Krähenberg’ and ‘Düthenberg’ mining fields. The Krähenbergstollen, also known as the Stemmecke-Stollen, was driven as a test tunnel to explore the ore deposit. In fact, it is known by this name amongst the local population; the name ‘Krähenbergstollen’ is used only in the authorisation documents. It was converted into a visitor tunnel by the Heimatverein Endorf (Endorf Local History Society) in cooperation with the District Government’s Mining Authority. Since it’s opening in 2019, Josef Müller and Heiner Hoff from the local history society have been offering guided tours. The reason this tunnel was chosen is that groundwater has seeped into the other tunnels, making further development impossible.

In addition to the Krähenbergstollen, the Stockumer Stollen is also open to visitors. The tunnel was opened in 2011 and made accessible via a stone staircase leading to the entrance. However, there are no guided tours here; visitors are free to explore the entrance at their leisure. Both tunnels are situated on the Bergbauwanderweg (Mining Trail), which opened back in 2004 and leads to various remnants of medieval mining.

The problem with this show mine is the lack of up-to-date information. The available information stops in 2019; the obvious assumption is that it was closed again due to the coronavirus. Unfortunately, the website of the Heimatverein Endorf e.V. was never created.
